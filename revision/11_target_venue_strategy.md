# Deliverable 11: Target-venue strategy

Checked 2026-10-28. "Fact" = verified on the venue's own page in this session; "Judgement" = my assessment.

## 1. International Journal of Prognostics and Health Management (IJPHM), PHM Society — **primary recommendation**
- **Facts (phmsociety.org/?p=3020, DOAJ):** no submission, article-processing, access or membership fees; open access, CC BY 3.0 US, authors keep copyright; full papers, technical briefs, communications (2-4 pp), surveys; Word and LaTeX templates; APA references; acknowledgement within 3 business days, decision typically 8-12 weeks; papers relying on undocumented proprietary methods may be rejected; indexed in Scopus, ESCI, DOAJ (per PHM Society / DOAJ pages). Submission: https://papers.phmsociety.org/index.php/ijphm/about/submissions
- **Judgement on fit:** good. Condition monitoring, diagnostics and evaluation practice for health monitoring are in scope; the paper's emphasis on threshold stability, drift and retraining is directly relevant to PHM practitioners. Recent relevant IJPHM papers were **not** checked in this session; do this before submitting and cite 2-3 of them.
- **What it needs:** official-SWaT rerun; IJPHM template; APA style (the revised draft already uses author-year).

## 2. Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR) — alternative if positioned as a methodology paper
- **Facts (jmlr.org/tmlr):** "emphasizes technical correctness over subjective significance"; does not accept submissions overlapping previously published work. Fee policy was not stated on the page fetched (widely reported as free; unverified here).
- **Judgement:** the correctness-first criterion suits a careful re-examination, but ML reviewers will compare against the TSAD critique literature closely; the novelty risk is higher here than at IJPHM. A third dataset would strengthen it.

## 3. Conference options (submission free, registration fee required to present)
- **IEEE INDIN 2027** — Lisbon, 10-14 May 2027 (fact, official site). Deadline not posted when checked. Judgement: good industrial-informatics fit; 6-page IEEE format would force cutting the paper roughly in half.
- **IEEE CASE 2027** — Linz, Aug 2027; one listing gave 1 Mar 2027 as paper deadline, another conflicted (unverified). Judgement: weaker fit.

## 4. Subscription journals (free if the non-open-access route is chosen)
- Elsevier *Engineering Applications of AI*, *Computers in Industry*: hybrid model per library/OA listings; author fee for the subscription route not verified on the publisher page. Judgement: plausible fits, slower and more selective.

## 5. Preprint
- **arXiv (cs.LG)**: free; first-time submitters need an endorsement. Judgement: post after Prof. Shah approves and the official-SWaT rerun is done; IJPHM's policy on preprints should be checked on the submission page.

## Not recommended
- Fee-based OA venues (MDPI journals, IEEE Access) unless a waiver is confirmed — no evidence of a waiver was found.
- Any venue chosen mainly because acceptance seems easy.
